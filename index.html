KNOW A LITTLE, DO A LOT

Good ideas for a greener life.

Simple changes that can make a real difference over time.

💧01 / WATER

Make every drop count.

Turn off the tap while brushing, take shorter showers, and report leaking taps when you notice them.

Save water
⚡02 / ENERGY

Switch off, save more.

Turn off lights and fans when leaving a room. Unplug chargers when you are not using them.

Save electricity
♻️03 / WASTE

Choose reusable first.

Carry a reusable bottle and bag. Reuse items when possible and sort waste for proper recycling.

Reduce waste
🌿04 / NATURE

Give nature some care.

Keep outdoor spaces clean, care for local plants, and choose walking or cycling for short trips when safe.

Protect nature

WHY ECO TRACKER?

A healthier planet begins with every one of us.

Eco Tracker is a simple student-friendly project that helps people notice their everyday environmental choices. It turns good intentions into small, trackable actions.

We believe sustainability is not about doing everything perfectly. It is about making better choices more often, learning as we go, and inspiring others to join in.
